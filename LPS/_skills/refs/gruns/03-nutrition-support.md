## 4 · Padrões de copy

- **Audience problem framed first** ("GLP-1s reduce appetite, which can make it tough to get all nutrients")
- **Mecanismo do produto** explicitado em 1 frase
- **Outcome temporal/sensorial** ("steady energy", "no horrible side effects")
- Tom: insider, "we get you" — escritura cúmplice
- **Stats específicos** intercalados ("21 essential vitamins", "60+ ingredients", "20 calories")
